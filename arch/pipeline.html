Session 6 · the pipeline

Four stages, and a folder
that runs them again

Data comes in on the left and a defensible story goes out on the right. Each stage has one job, writes one artefact, and hands it on. The skills and subagents in the middle row are what make the whole thing repeat on the next client without anyone remembering how it went the first time.

STAGE 01

Profile

Describe every column, every join risk, every row that is not a real transaction. Answer nothing yet.

data-profile

STAGE 02

Compute

Clean, join, calculate. Code produces every figure and writes it to the ledger with its method, sources and sample size.

analyst fact-ledger

STAGE 03

Attack

Recompute the headline independently, test three alternative explanations, say what the data cannot settle.

sceptic

STAGE 04

Compose

Five beats. Every sentence with a figure carries a fact id, and the build step substitutes the values at the end.

storywright story-craft

The point of stage three

A finding that survives an attack is worth charging for

Anyone can find a correlation in twenty seconds now. What a client pays for is being able to say what else you ruled out.

Why the ledger sits in the middle

The model plans and writes. It never types a number

Code computes, the ledger stores, the prose references by id. If a figure is wrong the fix is in the script, never in the document.

Why this is a folder

A method you can hand to someone else

The skills and agents carry no client, no sector and no dataset. The next engagement starts at stage one rather than at the beginning.
