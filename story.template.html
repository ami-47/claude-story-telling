Not settled

The recovery figure is a ceiling

A call log records the call, not the caller. Some of those people rang back an hour later and were answered. Nothing in these four files can tell you how many. Treat the recovered number as the most you could gain, not what you will gain.

Not settled

Enquiries are not patients

An answered call becomes an enquiry, and an enquiry sometimes becomes a patient. The conversion rate sits in the CRM, not in the phone log, so the revenue figure behind all of this is out of scope here.
