What this data cannot settle

No field in the export records whether a caller who reaches voicemail at 12:45 tries again later the same day and eventually gets through, or gives up on Northgate for good. The figure above is therefore a floor on the true weekly cost of the lunch gap, not the full number. The decision, add a second receptionist to the lunch rota, is worth taking on the numbers already in hand; how much it is worth in recovered new patients is something only a second measurement, after the change, can tell.
